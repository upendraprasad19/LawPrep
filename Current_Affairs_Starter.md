## 2. Major Supreme Court Judgments & Legal Developments

- **Right to Walk** — *Maniyar Iliyaz v. P. Ayyappan* (June 2026): the SC held that a **Fundamental Right to Walk** flows from the freedom of movement under **Article 19(1)(d)**; civic bodies must provide/maintain safe footpaths, prioritising pedestrians.
- **Homemakers' unpaid work** — *Shishu Pal v. Surjeet* (June 2026): SC called homemakers "Nation Builders" and recognised a new compensation head, **"loss of domestic care" (~₹30,000/month)**, in motor-accident compensation.
- **Human trafficking** — *Prajwala v. Union of India* (2026): adopted a **victim-centric** approach, holding rehabilitation to be part of dignity under **Article 21**, with fresh victim-protection guidelines.
- **Passive euthanasia:** SC permitted withdrawal of life support for a patient in a persistent vegetative state (March 2026). **(verify)**
- Other notable 2026 SC rulings addressed **maternity benefits for adoptive mothers**, **SC/ST status after religious conversion**, and appellate courts' duty to independently hear convicts on sentencing (*Mukesh Kumar Yadav v. State*). **(verify)**
- A trial court **discharged Arvind Kejriwal, Manish Sisodia and others** in the Delhi liquor-policy ("excise scam") case (February 2026). **(verify)**
- Separatist leader **Asiya Andrabi** was sentenced to life imprisonment for terror-related activities (March 2026). **(verify)**
- Convictions/death sentences were handed down in the **2020 Sathankulam custodial-death case** (Tamil Nadu) in 2026. **(verify)**
