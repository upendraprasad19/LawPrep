## 0. The one strategic idea

Run **CLAT** as the rigorous core engine (its skills are a *superset* of the other two), then **re-weight** for the two knowledge/quant exams as their dates approach. CLAT prep alone covers roughly **50% of BLET for free** (Current Affairs + Legal Aptitude + reading discipline). Your real admission target is **BLET / Patna Law College**; CLAT is a forcing-function and resource base, and CUET-PG (DU/BHU) is additive rigor + a genuine backup.

> ⚠️ **None of the three 2027 exam dates are officially confirmed** as of July 2026. The dashboard uses working estimates (CLAT 6 Dec 2026, CUET-PG ~mid-Mar 2027, BLET ~Apr 2027) — edit them the moment official notifications drop.

Prepare only "the CLAT way" and you'll be under-prepared on BLET's heavy static GK; prepare only "the BLET way" and CLAT's reading-speed demands will crush you. Run both tracks.

## 2. Section-wise syllabus (all three exams)

### CLAT 2027
- **English Language (20%)** — RC & inference, main-idea/argument ID, vocabulary-in-context, tone & attitude, grammar essentials, summary/conclusion. *No direct factual recall since the 2020 redesign — every section is passage-based.*
- **Current Affairs incl. GK (25%)** — daily newspaper, monthly CA compendium, national/international/legal news, schemes, awards/sports/books. Tested via 400–450-word passages.
- **Legal Reasoning (25%)** — principle→fact application (rule is given; you apply it, no prior law needed), constitutional/contract/tort/criminal principles, contemporary legal developments, landmark-judgment context.
- **Logical Reasoning (20%)** — critical reasoning (strengthen/weaken/assumption/inference), argument analysis, analogies, cause-effect, sequences. *Biggest rank-differentiator.*
- **Quantitative Techniques (10%)** — percentages, ratio, profit/loss, averages, DI, Class-10 algebra/mensuration. No calculators.

### CUET-PG LLB (COQP11)
- **English / Language** — comprehension, grammar, vocabulary, sentence correction.
- **GK & Current Affairs** — static + dynamic, basic legal awareness.
- **Computer Basics** — hardware vs software, I/O devices, MS Office, IT terminology. *One-time pass, not a habit.*
- **Quantitative Aptitude ★ (highest weight)** — %, profit/loss, SI/CI, averages, ratio, simplification, LCM/HCF, AP, mensuration (2D/3D), time-speed-distance.
- **Logical Reasoning** — series, analogies, coding-decoding, syllogisms, blood relations, direction sense, critical reasoning.
- *No Legal Reasoning section at all — ignore SEO articles that claim otherwise.*

### BLET (Patna Law College, 3-yr LL.B.)
- **General Knowledge — 50 marks (static)** — UN & international bodies, historical awareness, treaties, wars, world personalities, national/international awards, environmental & peace agreements, Indian polity & Constitution, geography, economy basics, science/tech GK.
- **Current Affairs — 25 marks** — last ~12 months: national, international, sports, awards, appointments, legal CA.
- **Legal Aptitude / Legal GK — 25 marks** — landmark judgments, CJI/SC facts, IPR basics, international law, law of crimes (IPC→BNS), legal maxims, constitutional provisions, legal terminology.

## 3. The staged plan (your calendar)

**Stage 1 — Foundation, CLAT-led · Now → Aug 2026**
Build the daily engine (5–7 focused hours as 5–7 tasks); 30 min Hindu/IE editorials; 20–50 daily MCQs; one core book per section; **start monthly CA notes now** (the one thing you cannot backload); take a diagnostic full CLAT mock; register CLAT ~1 Aug 2026.
*Advance when:* concepts covered in all five sections and you can finish a mock within 120 min (even at low accuracy).

**Stage 2 — CLAT Peak · Sep → Dec 2026**
Ramp mocks 1–2/wk → 2–3/wk (Nov) → alternate-day (final fortnight); analyse **every** mock 2–3 hrs with the 5-type error log; practise on paper; register CUET-PG (~2nd wk Dec); sit CLAT ~6 Dec.
*Benchmarks:* Legal ~80–85%, English/Logical ~70–75%, Quant ~70%+, rising GK. A ~90–105/120 total is a good rigor gauge.

**Stage 3a — CUET-PG Pivot · Dec 2026 → Mar 2027**
Dedicated Quant block with speed drills (CUET-PG's biggest scoring block); single weekend on Computer Basics; keep GK/CA/English/Reasoning momentum; sit CUET-PG ~mid-Mar.

**Stage 3b — BLET Pivot (primary target) · Mar → Apr 2027**
Make **static GK** the priority (Lucent + Manorama); switch legal prep to legal-GK memorisation; down-prioritise Quant; **no negative marking → attempt all 100**; practise analogue papers (Panjab Univ LLB, MH CET Law 3-yr) + the Scribd 2020–23 set; submit BLET application ~Feb 2027; verify official 2027 pattern the moment it drops.

**Re-baseline triggers:** any CLAT 2027 pattern change → discard old material; BLET 2027 notification differing from 50/25/25 @ 90 min → re-weight Stage 3b; Legal/GK mock accuracy plateauing below benchmark for 3+ mocks → sectional drills + error-log review.

## 4. Mock discipline (where ranks are actually made)

- Target ~**30–50 full-length mocks**, but the number matters far less than **analysis** — spend 2–3 hrs dissecting each (as long as the test itself).
- Maintain an **error log** tagging every mistake by type: (1) didn't know the rule/fact, (2) knew but misapplied, (3) misread the passage, (4) careless/marking error, (5) guess under time pressure. *(The dashboard's Mock Arena does exactly this.)*
- CLAT & BLET are offline — practise on paper, not only on screen.
- Common pitfalls: over-relying on mocks without concept revision; ignoring feedback; obsessing over peer rank instead of your own accuracy trend; backloading Current Affairs (fatal — it cannot be crammed).

## 5. Section-wise resources (2026 editions)

**Legal Reasoning / Aptitude** — A.P. Bhardwaj *Legal Awareness & Legal Reasoning*; *Universal's Guide to CLAT & LL.B.*; Harsh Gagrani *Pearson Guide to CLAT*. For BLET add **legal-GK**: *Legal GK for Competitive Exams* (Universal), Arihant *Chapter-wise Objective Question Bank for Law*.

**English** — Norman Lewis *Word Power Made Easy* (vocab, supplement); Wren & Martin (grammar reference); S.P. Bakshi / R.S. Aggarwal *Objective General English*. Core habit: 20–30 min/day Hindu/IE editorials.

**GK / Current Affairs** — daily Hindu/IE; monthly *CLAT Express*, free **12 Minutes to CLAT CA Compendium**, Hitbullseye *Bull Dose*; **static GK for BLET: Lucent's GK + Manorama Yearbook**. Feeds: PIB, Supreme Court site, PRS Legislative Research.

**Logical Reasoning** — M.K. Pandey *Analytical Reasoning*; R.S. Aggarwal *A Modern Approach to Logical Reasoning*; GMAT Official Guide critical-reasoning set (strong analogue for CLAT's slant).

**Quantitative** — R.S. Aggarwal *Quantitative Aptitude*; NCERT Maths Cl. 8–10; BSC *Data Interpretation*; M. Tyra *Magical Book on Quicker Maths* (speed — vital for CUET-PG's tight 90 min).

**Computer Basics (CUET-PG only)** — any Arihant/Lucent "Computer Awareness for Competitive Exams" — one thorough pass.

**YouTube/apps** — Law Prep Tutorial, LegalEdge (Toprankers), Law Wallah (PW), Unacademy Law, iQuanta. Free mock/CA tiers on most.

## 7. Caveats to keep front of mind

- BLET **past papers are genuinely scarce** (reportedly collected back after the exam) — lean on analogue 3-yr LLB papers.
- BLET **duration unverified** (90 min best-sourced; one aggregator says 2 hrs + 5 sections — treat as unverified).
- CUET-PG has **changed format before** (105→90 min between 2024–25) — re-verify the section list when the 2027 syllabus PDF drops.
- CLAT UG **cannot** admit you to Patna Law College's 3-yr LLB — its value is rigor + resources only.
- **Age limit removed** for Patna Law College LL.B. entrance — "a candidate of any age may apply."
