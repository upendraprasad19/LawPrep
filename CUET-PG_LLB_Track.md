# CUET-PG LLB (COQP11): Third Track — Research, Past Papers & Resource List

*Companion document to `CLAT 2027 and Patna University BLET.md`. Read that one first — this one only covers what's different about CUET-PG.*

## TL;DR — the one thing that changes your prep

**CUET-PG LLB has no Legal Reasoning section at all**, and multiple independent breakdowns confirm it: the paper is English/Hindi, GK & Current Affairs, Computer Basics, Quantitative Aptitude, and Logical Reasoning — with **Quantitative Aptitude carrying the highest weightage** of any section, and Computer Basics as a small distinct section neither CLAT nor BLET tests at all. This is the mirror image of BLET (all GK/legal, zero quant) and a sharp departure from CLAT (legal reasoning is 25%, quant is the lightest section at 10%). Practically: your CLAT/BLET prep covers English, GK/Current Affairs, and general Logical Reasoning for this exam too — but **Quant needs real dedicated time here that your other two tracks don't require**, and Computer Basics needs a light, one-time pass, not ongoing study.

One SEO-templated source claims "ignoring Legal Reasoning can drastically reduce your score" for CUET-PG LLB — this directly contradicts the corroborated, exam-code-specific analysis (confirmed against actual 2025 shift-by-shift paper review) and should be disregarded; it reads as boilerplate copied from a CLAT article.

## Exam pattern at a glance
- **75 MCQs, 90 minutes, 300 marks total, +4 correct / −1 incorrect**, computer-based (online), single unified paper — no separate sections you choose between.
- Sections: English/Hindi (comprehension, grammar, vocabulary), GK & Current Affairs (static + dynamic), Computer Basics (hardware/software, MS Office, input-output devices — mostly factual/concept recall), Quantitative Aptitude (percentages, profit-loss, SI/CI, averages, ratio-proportion, simplification, LCM/HCF, Arithmetic Progression, mensuration — 2D/3D geometry), Logical Reasoning.
- NTA does not publish an official chapter-wise weightage breakdown — the above is aggregated from multiple independent shift-by-shift analyses of the actual 2025/2026 papers, not a single unverified source.
- 2027 cycle: registration expected second week of December 2026, exam expected March 2027 (most sources point to the 2nd–4th week of March; CUET-PG LLB 2026 was actually held **8 March 2026**, so treat mid-March as your working target date).

|---|---|---|---|

| Duration | 120 min | 90 min | ~90 min (unverified) |

| Negative marking | −0.25 per wrong | −1 per wrong | **None** |
| Mode | Offline (OMR) | Online (CBT) | Offline (OMR) |
| Legal Reasoning | 25% — passage/principle-application | **Not tested** | 25% — legal-GK recall |
| GK/Current Affairs | 25% | Yes, significant | 75% (GK 50 + CA 25) |
| English | 20% | Yes | Bilingual paper, not separately scored |
| Logical Reasoning | 20% | Yes | Implicit in legal aptitude section |
| Quantitative | 10% (lightest) | **Highest weightage** | Not tested |
| Computer Basics | Not tested | Yes (small section) | Not tested |
| Skill emphasis | Inference, passage-reading, elimination | Factual + calculation speed, less inference | Static recall, no penalty for guessing |
| Admission route | 5-year integrated NLUs (not your route) | DU, BHU, and 190+ universities' 3-yr LLB | Patna Law College 3-yr LLB (your primary target) |

**What this means for sequencing:** your existing CLAT-anchored prep (Aug–Nov 2026) already builds English, GK/CA, and general reasoning muscle for all three exams. The two things worth adding to that window specifically *because* of CUET-PG: (1) a Class 10-level Quant refresh with speed drills — same content as CLAT's Quant section but you now need it sharper since it's CUET-PG's biggest scoring block, and (2) a single-pass Computer Basics revision (a weekend, not a weekly habit) covering hardware/software terms, input/output devices, and basic MS Office — this is unique to CUET-PG and easy to max out cheaply.

## DU and BHU — the headline targets

- **Delhi University Faculty of Law** admits through three centres: Campus Law Centre (CLC), Law Centre I, Law Centre II. DU LLB 2025 Round 1 cutoffs (out of 300): **CLC UR 213, OBC 187, SC 167, ST 135, EWS 196**; Law Centre I UR 188, OBC 167, SC 143, ST 95, EWS 177; Law Centre II UR 193, OBC 169, SC 146, ST 94, EWS 180. For 2026, DU's expected general-category cutoff is quoted around the **98–98.25 percentile** mark. DU offers 3,000+ seats across its law centres combined and is widely regarded as the most prestigious and most affordable government 3-year LLB in India. Admission after CUET-PG runs through DU's own CSAS-PG counselling portal — you apply separately to DU after your CUET-PG score is out.
- **Banaras Hindu University (BHU) Faculty of Law**: expected 2026 cutoffs (out of 300) — **General 205–210+, OBC 190–195+, EWS 195–200+, SC 170–175+, ST 140–145+**. BHU runs a two-stage process too: CUET-PG score first, then BHU's own application and counselling (bhu.ac.in). BHU is known for consistently higher cutoffs than DU's Law Centre I/II given its smaller seat count and strong regional draw.
- **Other reputable CUET-PG LLB universities** worth a look as further backups: Allahabad University, Central University of Haryana, Babasaheb Bhimrao Ambedkar University (BBAU) Lucknow, University of Jammu, DHSGSU. Roughly 10+ institutions participate in CUET-PG LLB (COQP11) each year among the ~190 total CUET-PG participating universities across all subjects; law is a small subset.

## Study materials — only what's specific to CUET-PG

Your CLAT-track English, GK/Current Affairs, and Logical Reasoning resources carry over directly (see the CLAT+BLET document). Adding here only the two CUET-PG-specific gaps:

**Quantitative Aptitude (now your highest-priority section for this exam):**
- *Quantitative Aptitude for Competitive Examinations* — R.S. Aggarwal (core reference, same book already on your CLAT list — just go deeper here: full chapters on Mensuration, Time-Speed-Distance, and Arithmetic Progression, which recur in nearly every 2025 shift)
- NCERT Maths, Classes 8–10 (fundamentals refresh)
- *Magical Book on Quicker Maths* — M. Tyra (speed technique, useful given CUET-PG's tighter 90-minute window across 75 questions vs CLAT's more generous pacing)

**Computer Basics (one-time pass, not ongoing):**
- Any standard "Computer Awareness for Competitive Exams" compilation (Arihant or Lucent both publish one) — cover hardware vs software, input/output devices, MS Office basics, and generic IT terminology. This section rewards a single thorough revision far more than repeated practice.

**CUET-PG-specific coaching content (for pattern familiarity, not new syllabus):**
- CLAT NLTI's CUET-PG LLB blog series (syllabus breakdowns, cutoff analysis, PYQ guides) — useful for pattern-tracking, treat as secondary/analysis source rather than primary study material: https://www.clatnlti.com
- Law Prep Tutorial and LegalEdge (already on your CLAT list) both maintain CUET-PG LLB-specific playlists alongside their CLAT content.

## Recommendations
- **Don't open a new daily habit for CUET-PG** — your CLAT-driven English/GK/Reasoning routine already serves it. The only new addition to your calendar: a focused Quant block once CLAT is behind you (Dec 2026 onward) and a single weekend for Computer Basics sometime in January–February 2027, before the March exam.
- **Register the moment the window opens** (~2nd week Dec 2026) — CUET-PG's per-university counselling (CSAS-PG for DU, BHU's own process) rewards early, careful registration since correction windows are typically narrower than CLAT's.
- **Treat DU and BHU cutoffs as a stretch target, not the primary plan** — the cutoffs above (DU general ~213/300 at CLC, BHU general ~205-210/300) sit meaningfully above BLET's projected 65-75/100 cutoff band, so this track is additive rigor and a genuine backup, not a replacement for your Patna Law College plan.
- When the official 2027 CUET-PG LLB syllabus PDF is released (check https://pgcuet.samarth.ac.in or exams.nta.nic.in/CUET-PG around Dec 2026–Jan 2027), re-verify the section list against this document — CUET-PG's format has changed before (105→90 minute duration cut between 2024 and 2025) and could shift again.

## Caveats
- NTA does not publish an official chapter-wise weightage table for COQP11; the section priorities above are triangulated from multiple independent post-exam analyses, not a single official figure.

- Cutoff figures are prior-year actuals/expected-2026 estimates; 2027 cutoffs will depend on that year's applicant pool and paper difficulty and should be re-checked once results are out.
