# Law Prep content review task board

Started 31 October 2026. Status: active. Requested by the owner: deep correctness research, generate the missing explanations, and identify recent question banks that are possible additions.

## Scope and working rules

- Review all 1,203 questions, including the 711 existing explanations; draft the 492 missing explanations. Check the related 41 lessons, 65 curriculum entries and four deck structures for factual or exam-fit issues. These counts describe original records, not approved learning units.
- Prioritise CLAT as the main product, with Patna BLET as the owner's use case and CUET-PG LLB as the existing third route. Legacy PU/BLET labels are claims requiring verification.
- Preserve the sealed original import. Corrections and explanations are separate, source-bound proposals. No automatic database overwrite, content publication, new hosted schema, purchases, account creation, commits or pushes.
- Use official papers and final/revised keys first. Passage questions must be explained from the supplied passage/principles; distinguish an exam's intended answer from current general-law knowledge. Date-check legal and current-affairs claims.
- Every item gets a recorded outcome: confirmed, correction proposed, ambiguous, source/key missing, or not yet reviewed. A generated explanation does not count as independently checked. Unresolved items remain clearly blocked.
- Candidate banks are a researched list only. Record official access, year, paper/subject code, availability of final keys, overlap with current records, relevance, and reuse restrictions. Do not equate a download link with permission to republish.
