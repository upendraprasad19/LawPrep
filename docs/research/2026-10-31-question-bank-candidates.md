## Acceptance checklist for a later import proposal

Record publisher, programme/cycle/date/shift/set/language, lawful source access, reuse terms, immutable source hash, transcription/image fidelity, official Question-ID/option mapping, latest key and withdrawals. Compare exact stems, near paraphrases, passages and bilingual equivalents. Keep new source questions private drafts until independent editorial review. A download link or absence of a year label is insufficient evidence of novelty or publication permission.
